Confidence does not always arrive before you begin. Sometimes it shows up after the first sentence, first note, or first step. This short reset can help you reach that starting point.

## Minute one: slow your breathing

Breathe in gently, then make your exhale a little longer. Repeat without forcing it. A slower rhythm gives your attention somewhere steady to land.

## Minute two: remember your preparation

Name three things you already did to get ready. Maybe you practiced, reviewed your notes, or asked a useful question. Preparation still counts even when you feel nervous.

## Minute three: choose your opening

Think only about the first action. Picture yourself saying the opening line or walking to the right spot. You can handle the next step after that.

Being nervous and being ready can happen at the same time.
