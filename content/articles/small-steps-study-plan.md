When everything feels important, deciding where to begin can be the hardest part. A small-step study plan helps by turning one big list into a few choices you can make today.

## Start with what is real

sdajsdklaWrite down the assignments and tests you already know about. Add their due dates, then circle the two items that need attention first. You do not need to organize the entire month before you begin.

## Make the next step specific

“Work on science” is difficult to start because it could mean many things. “Read two pages and write three notes” gives you a clear finish line. A useful next step should fit into about twenty minutes.

## Leave room for life

A plan is meant to help you, not control every minute. Give yourself breaks, time with friends or family, and a little extra space for surprises.

Try this simple rhythm:

* Choose one priority.
* Work for a short, focused block.
* Take a real break away from the screen.
* Decide on the next small step.

Progress can look quiet. Finishing one thoughtful step is still progress.
