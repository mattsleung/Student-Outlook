Here is a weekend challenge that needs only a few pages and a little imagination: trade short stories with a friend, then talk about what surprised you.

## Pick a playful prompt

Choose one prompt for everyone. It could be “A library book starts giving advice” or “The school bus takes one impossible turn.” A shared starting point makes the different results even more fun.

## Keep it short

Set a limit of two pages or thirty minutes of writing. The goal is to finish an idea, not produce a perfect story.

## Respond like a reader

After swapping stories, share a favorite moment and one question you still have. Focus on curiosity instead of giving a score.

You can write by hand, type, or tell the story aloud. The best format is the one that helps everyone join in.
