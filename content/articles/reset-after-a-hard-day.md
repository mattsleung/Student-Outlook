Some days feel heavy from the beginning. Maybe a plan fell apart, a conversation went badly, or your energy simply disappeared. A hard day can be real without becoming the story of your whole week.

## Name what happened

You do not have to solve every feeling. Start by putting simple words to it: “That test was frustrating,” or “I felt left out today.” Naming the moment can make it feel more manageable.

## Choose one kind thing

Drink water, stretch, listen to a favorite song, or step outside for a few minutes. Small acts of care tell your brain that the difficult moment is not the only thing happening.

## Begin again without a perfect plan

Resetting does not mean pretending the day was easy. It means giving tomorrow a fair chance. Pick one helpful action for the morning and let that be enough for now.

You are allowed to have an unfinished day. Rest can be part of moving forward.
